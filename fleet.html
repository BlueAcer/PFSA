FLEET QUESTIONS

Before you
book a van.

Can you repair large delivery vans?+

Vehicle dimensions, access, equipment and paint facilities are checked before acceptance. Please send the make, model and approximate height when enquiring.

Can you collect vans from our depot?+

Collection or delivery can be discussed individually, subject to location, workshop capacity and appropriate insurance arrangements. It is not automatically included.

Can repairs be invoiced monthly?+

Trade account terms may be discussed after a successful initial job and any required account approval.

Do you work directly for Amazon or other national delivery companies?+

We welcome enquiries from independent delivery partners and fleet managers, but we do not claim to be an approved Amazon or national carrier repairer. Confirm any required supplier authorisation before booking.
