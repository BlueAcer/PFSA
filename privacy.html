Privacy notice.

How website enquiry information is handled.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who we are

PRECISION FS AUTOS, Unit 5, St Bartholomew Building, Off Nelson Street, Bolton, Greater Manchester, BL3 2AH, UK. Contact: contact@precisionfsautos.co.uk.

What we collect

If you complete our contact form or send us an email, we may receive your name, contact details, vehicle information and message, including any repair details you choose to share.

Why we use it

We use this information to answer enquiries, assess repair requests, provide quotations and communicate about potential services. Our usual lawful basis for handling routine enquiries is taking steps at your request before entering a contract and/or legitimate interests, as applicable.

Sharing and retention

Website enquiries are processed by our web hosting provider (Vercel) and, once configured, our transactional email provider (Resend). We do not sell enquiry information. We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for enquiries, quotations, business records and any applicable legal obligations. Specific retention periods should be documented in the business's operational data protection policy.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, restriction or deletion of your personal data when the law applies. Contact us at contact@precisionfsautos.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Cookies and analytics

This initial website package does not set marketing or analytics cookies. The enquiry form uses standard browser controls and a serverless handler; it does not itself set a session cookie. If analytics, maps embeds or advertising cookies are added later, this notice and any required consent controls must be updated first.

Security

We take appropriate care with website enquiries. Do not submit payment card numbers, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information through the enquiry form.

Please review and approve this notice against your actual business practices and hosting configuration before publication.
