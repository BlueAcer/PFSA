IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

No shortcuts
with high voltage.

EV and hybrid vehicles can retain dangerous high-voltage energy even when switched off. Body and accident repairs must be planned around manufacturer guidance and competent personnel.

We do not claim manufacturer accreditation or the capability to undertake every EV body repair. Any repair involving high-voltage exposure, damaged batteries or specialist procedures is accepted only if appropriate competence and controls can be confirmed; otherwise we will advise seeking a suitable specialist.
